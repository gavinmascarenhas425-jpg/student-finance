        What is a budget?

        A budget is a plan for your money: what comes in, and where it goes. A popular guide is about 50% for needs, 30% for wants and 20% for saving. With pocket money, set your savings aside first, then split the rest.

        Needs vs wants

        Needs are things you can't do without, like food, travel to college and course books. Wants are nice extras. Before buying, ask: "Will I be okay without this for a month?"

        Why start saving early

        Saving ₹500 a month for 10 years at an example rate of 7% a year grows to about ₹86,000, though you only put in ₹60,000. The earlier you start, the more time your money has to grow. Rates are not guaranteed.

        Stay safe with UPI

        Never share your UPI PIN or an OTP with anyone. You never need a PIN to receive money, so a "request" that asks for one is a scam. Check the name before you pay.

        Build a small safety fund

        Keep a little money aside, even ₹2,000 to ₹5,000, for surprises like a phone repair or a missed bus. Keep it separate from your spending money.

        Be careful with "pay later"

        Credit cards and "buy now, pay later" offers can charge high interest if you don't pay on time. Use them only for things you need, and pay in full.
